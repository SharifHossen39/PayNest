## Summary

- **Findings:** 11 change recommendations and 1 explicit preserve recommendation.
- **Estimated reduction if all change recommendations are accepted:** **859 words**, from 7,043 to approximately **6,184 words** (**12.2%**).
- **Length target:** No target was provided; the recommendations optimize hierarchy, single-source-of-truth behavior, and scanability rather than pursuing a fixed word count.
- **Comprehension trade-offs:** The proposed cuts remove repeated verification statements, not examples, journeys, diagrams, or endpoint payloads. The main risk is weakening traceability while merging §§8 and 24; preserve the FR, BR, AC, and test identifiers or an equivalent cross-reference scheme during that merge.
- **Overall assessment:** The PRD has complete, well-scaffolded content and a strong opening. Its largest structural opportunity is to replace repeated requirement inventories with one authoritative feature contract, then bring learning outcomes, transactional integrity, and architecture closer to the top of the document.
